# Future ideas — a park where friends meet

Recorded from the owner's discussion with Claude, 2026-10-11.

The simple current page is a useful canvas for brainstorming. Preserve that
foundation while exploring these possibilities later:

- Park furniture that invites games: chess tables, ping-pong tables and other
  recognizable places to play.
- Entering the site feels like arriving in a park and walking up to a game area.
- A friend stands nearby and waves you over to your shared table.
- Seasonal surroundings and shared game history give repeated visits continuity.

These are possibilities, not committed features or Sprint 2 requirements. Explore
them after Arne and Harald can reliably play across devices. Keep a direct way to
reach ongoing games, including for touch, keyboard and reduced-motion use.
